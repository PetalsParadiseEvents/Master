About Petals Paradise Events

Petals Paradise Events is a family-run event decor and party rental company based in Ashburn, Virginia. Since 2025 we have helped families across Loudoun County, Fairfax County and the wider DMV celebrate birthdays, baby showers, graduations, weddings and South Asian ceremonies such as Haldi, Mehandi and Seemantham.

What we do

  • Full-service decor: balloon garlands and arches, floral arrangements, custom backdrops and themed setups.
  • Rentals: chairs, tables, tents, backdrops, marquee letters, neon signs and ceremony decor.
  • Delivery, setup and pickup across Northern Virginia, DC and Maryland.

Why customers choose us

  • One team, one call for every celebration.
  • Transparent pricing and online booking at petalsparadiseevents.com.
  • Petals Rewards: earn 5 points per $1 on rentals.
