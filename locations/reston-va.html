Proudly Serving Reston, VA

Reston, Virginia stands apart as one of America's first planned communities — a city conceived by visionary Robert E. Simon with the idea that people of all backgrounds could live, work, and thrive together. That original spirit of inclusive community remains alive today, and it's one of the reasons why Petals Paradise Events is honored to serve Reston's residents and businesses. Whether you're hosting a rooftop corporate mixer at Reston Town Center, a waterside gathering near Lake Anne Plaza, or a backyard birthday bash in the North Point or Hunters Woods neighborhoods, we deliver the rental essentials you need to make it happen beautifully.

The Reston Town Center is the beating heart of local social life — a sophisticated open-air destination where annual traditions like the Reston Multicultural Festival, the beloved Town Center Oktoberfest, and Fourth of July fireworks draw thousands of community members together. For events held near this iconic hub, our tent rentals, cocktail tables, and elegant seating options are frequently in high demand. The Hyatt Regency Reston is another preferred venue for upscale galas and corporate receptions, and we are well-acquainted with the logistics needed to deliver and set up seamlessly within such polished environments.

Reston's neighborhoods reflect its incredible diversity. From the tech-industry families of Sutton Ridge and Halstead Square to the creative professionals who gather at the Reston Farmers Market on weekends, this community celebrates life's milestones with genuine enthusiasm. The W&OD Trail corridor also makes Reston a favorite for outdoor celebrations, where our canopy tents and folding tables create wonderful al fresco event spaces. We've supplied rentals for graduation parties in South Lakes, baby showers in Lake Anne, and cultural ceremonies throughout the 20190, 20191, and 20194 zip codes — and we bring that local familiarity to every delivery.

Reston's robust South Asian community is one of our most cherished client groups.
